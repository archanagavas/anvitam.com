```
Isolated spread footings are guaranteed to fail on deep black cotton soil.

When expansive clay swells during monsoons, it lifts the exterior columns.
When it desiccates in April, it drops them.

Differential settlement exceeds 25mm.
Doors jam. Windows crack. Masonry shears.

Here is our structural detailing standard:

1. Bored cast-in-situ piles with 2x shaft bulb under-reams.

4. Pervious gravel aprons with geotextile wrap around the perimeter.

5. French drains routing subsurface water to gravity-fed storage ponds.

Engineering is about understanding ground realities.

You can build a foundation that lasts 100 years on any soil.
```

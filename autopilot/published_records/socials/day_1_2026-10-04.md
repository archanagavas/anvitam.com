## Asset 2: [11:30 AM] Show Your Work (Technical Proof) — Stabilizer ratio math: Lime vs Portland cement

### LinkedIn / Longform (SLAY Format)
```
Stabilizing earth with Portland cement is often a massive mistake.

Contractors love it because it sets fast.

They dump 10% Ordinary Portland Cement into red laterite.
They ram it into forms.
They call it a sustainable wall.

Two monsoons pass.

The cement traps internal vapor.
The wall cannot breathe.
Surface spalling and white salt efflorescence destroy the finish.

The result?
A degraded wall that defeats the purpose of natural building.

Here is what soil physics actually demands:

1. Use 5-7% hydraulic lime instead of cement.
Lime allows continuous vapor permeability through the wall.

2. Verify subsoil clay content below 18%.
Excess clay causes micro-fissures during drying shrinkage.

3. Test moisture using the field drop test.
A ball dropped from 1 meter should shatter into 4 equal pieces.

4. Compact with pneumatic rammers at 6-8 bar.
Manual tamping cannot achieve 2.0+ N/mm² structural strength.

5. Cure slowly under damp hessian for 21 days.
Lime carbonation requires controlled moisture, not rapid sun drying.

Biology and physics always beat contractor shortcuts.

You deserve an estate that breathes naturally for generations.

Have you tested the soil on your land yet?
```

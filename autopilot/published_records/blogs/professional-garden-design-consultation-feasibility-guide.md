If your water flow isn't mapped, your beautiful stone paving will likely heave or crack within two monsoons.</p><p>We use a simple calculation: infiltration rate divided by local rainfall intensity. ### Frequently Asked Questions (Top Search Queries)

**Q: How much does a professional soil test cost?**

A professional soil analysis usually ranges from ₹3,000 to ₹10,000 depending on the depth of the lab test. It is a tiny fraction of your overall budget that prevents thousands in wasted plant costs.

**Q: Do I really need an architect for a garden?**

If your garden involves structural changes, drainage, or permanent hardscape, yes. An architect ensures that your garden doesn't compromise the safety of your home's foundation or roof.

**Q: Why is my plant survival rate so low?**

Usually, it is a mismatch between the plant's biological needs and the site's soil health or water-logging. A soil and hydrology audit identifies these imbalances before you invest in more plants.

**Q: What is the biggest mistake people make in terrace gardening?**

Most people ignore the structural weight limit and waterproofing integrity. Using heavy soil or improper drainage layers can lead to permanent damage to the building's roof slab.

**Q: How do I know if my land is suitable for a food forest?**

A food forest requires specific soil biology and moisture retention capabilities. A percolation test and organic carbon mapping will tell you if your soil can support the necessary plant guilds.

**Q: How often should I conduct a landscape audit?**

Ideally, conduct a full site audit before any construction. If you have an existing mature garden, an annual health check-up including soil nutrient mapping is sufficient for maintenance.

**Q: Can I DIY my site analysis?**

You can perform basic observations, but professional instruments like laser scanners and soil moisture sensors are necessary for accurate data. DIY tests are often too imprecise for major investments.

**Q: What if my budget is limited for a consultant?**

Even a single hour of expert consultation can save you from catastrophic design errors. Prioritize the initial feasibility report over expensive finishes or decorative elements.

**Q: Does permaculture really save money in the long run?**

Yes, by reducing dependence on external fertilizers, synthetic pesticides, and high-volume water usage. A well-designed ecological system becomes self-sustaining and cheaper to manage over time.

**Q: How long does a landscape feasibility study take?**

Depending on the site size, a comprehensive audit takes between 1 to 3 weeks. This includes laboratory processing time for soil samples and data compilation for the masterplan.

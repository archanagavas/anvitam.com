### Practical Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What is keyline drainage and how does it work?**

Keyline drainage is a technique used to manage water runoff and prevent waterlogging in areas with high rainfall or poor drainage. It involves creating a network of shallow channels and swales that collect and redirect water away from the building site.

**Q: How much does keyline drainage cost?**

The cost of keyline drainage can vary depending on the size of the building site and the complexity of the drainage system. However, on average, the cost of keyline drainage can range from ₹500 to ₹2,000 per square meter.

**Q: How does keyline drainage prevent foundation cracks?**

Keyline drainage prevents foundation cracks by reducing the amount of water that enters the soil and minimizing the risk of shrinkage and expansion. By collecting and redirecting water away from the building site, keyline drainage helps to maintain a stable moisture level in the soil.

**Q: What are the benefits of keyline drainage?**

The benefits of keyline drainage include reduced risk of foundation cracks and structural problems, minimized risk of erosion and soil degradation, improved soil health and fertility, increased water efficiency and reduced water waste, and cost-effectiveness compared to conventional drainage systems.

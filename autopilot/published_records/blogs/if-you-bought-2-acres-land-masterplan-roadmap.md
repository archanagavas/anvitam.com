### Practical Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How do I ensure my glamping pods remain moisture-free during the Alibaug monsoon?**

We elevate all structures on a 450mm basalt stone plinth to create a physical break between the soil and the floor. By using breathable lime-based mortars rather than cement, we allow the walls to wick moisture outward, preventing internal dampness.

**Q: Can I use modular construction in a CRZ-zoned area?**

Yes, provided the structures are designed as temporary or non-permanent installations that do not use permanent concrete foundations. Our practice specializes in lightweight, modular designs that meet coastal regulatory requirements while remaining structurally robust.

**Q: Is laterite stone better than brick for my foundation?**

Laterite is significantly superior in the Alibaug climate because it oxidizes into a hardened, weather-resistant stone that naturally regulates moisture. Bricks are porous and often fail under prolonged monsoon exposure without expensive chemical waterproofing.

**Q: How do I calculate the water requirements for my glamping resort?**

We calculate a water budget based on guest occupancy and landscape needs, focusing on rainwater harvesting via roof catchment and graywater recycling through root-zone wetlands. This creates a closed-loop system that reduces dependence on external tankers.

**Q: What is the best way to orient my pods for maximum comfort?**

Pods should be oriented to face the prevailing sea breezes while minimizing exposure to the intense afternoon western sun. We use our microclimate layout and orientation studio to map the sun path against your specific plot contours.

**Q: How can I start the planning process if my land has poor soil quality?**

Poor soil is not a barrier; it is a design parameter. We use soil amendment strategies, including the planting of native Casuarina windbreaks and the construction of swales, to stabilize the ground before any structure is placed.

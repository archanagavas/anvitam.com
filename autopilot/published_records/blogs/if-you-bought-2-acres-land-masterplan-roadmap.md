### Practical Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I use rammed earth in the high-humidity climate of Alibaug?**

Yes, provided the rammed earth walls are protected by deep roof overhangs and a raised basalt plinth. Proper stabilization and hydrophobic lime renders ensure long-term durability.

**Q: Are chemical-free termite barriers actually effective?**

Physical barriers, such as graded stone beds and copper mesh combined with biological deterrents like neem oil, are more effective and longer-lasting than synthetic chemicals.

**Q: How often should I inspect for termites?**

Inspect your foundation perimeter every six months, specifically after the monsoon season, to look for mud tubes or moisture-affected areas on your plinth.

**Q: Is it safe to build a wood-based home in Alibaug?**

Yes, if the timber is properly seasoned, treated with borate or natural oil baths, and elevated from the ground using stone or metal piers.

**Q: What is the best foundation type for expansive soil?**

A stone-rubble trench foundation with a hydraulic lime-pozzolan mortar provides the necessary flexibility to accommodate soil movement without cracking.

**Q: How do I start planning my Alibaug farmstead?**

Begin with a comprehensive site analysis. This includes hydrological mapping, soil testing, and determining microclimates before placing a single stone on the land.

# human-tone: Write Copy That Doesn't Read Like a Bot

## What to Cut (AI Outreach Tells):
- "I hope this email finds you well"
- "I am reaching out to introduce our cutting-edge..."
- "empirical technical case study", "diurnal temperature swing", "active mechanical refrigeration"
- Formal titles: "Dear Editorial Team", "Dear Founder" -> Replace with "Hi [Name]," or "Hi [Company] team,"
- Inanimate objects taking human actions
- Throat clearing and verbose self-introductions

## What to Aim For:
- Warm, concise, conversational tone of a practicing architect (Ar. Archana Gavas).
- Short sentences and paragraphs.
- Specific observations about the client's actual property.
- Simple, friendly sign-off: "Best," or "Warmly," followed by Archana.

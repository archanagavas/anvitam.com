# Cold Email Personalization

## Rules of Personalization:
1. **Never use generic compliments** ("I hope this finds you well", "I was impressed by your company").
2. **Anchor to physical landscape or design intent**: For an estate or eco-retreat, mention their specific topography, rainfall, microclimate, or architectural character (e.g. desert stone, olive groves, coastal breezes, red laterite soil).
3. **Keep body copy between 60 and 120 words**.
4. **Use Plain Text**: No bulleted proposal decks, no complex tables, no bold headers inside cold outreach.
